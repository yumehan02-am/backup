# 010 画像生成プロンプト

共通制約: 商品画像、商品名、価格、型番、ロゴ、日本語テキストは背景に入れない。人物は服を着た自然な生活シーン。顔アップ、下着姿、露出、身体悩みの直接描写は禁止。

## 010_title_bg.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Rakuten content title background, later composed as 1600 x 900
Scene/backdrop: bright morning entryway and closet area before going out, wide pants on a hanger, small handbag, soft natural light
Subject: tasteful everyday preparation scene for a woman in her 40s-50s, only hands or back silhouette may appear
Style/medium: women's magazine editorial photography
Composition/framing: generous clean negative space for Japanese title text, no in-image text
Lighting/mood: light, fresh, slightly humorous but elegant
Avoid: toilet room, product photo, underwear scene, face close-up, logos, text
