# 012 画像生成プロンプト

共通制約: 商品画像、商品名、価格、型番、ロゴ、日本語テキストは背景に入れない。服を着た女性の自然な生活シーン。顔アップ、下着姿、露出、身体悩みの直接描写は禁止。トイレ内を直接描写しすぎず、個室前後や心の中の作戦会議として表現する。

## 012_title_bg.png

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: Rakuten content title background, later composed as 1600 x 900
Scene/backdrop: light women's magazine watercolor illustration, mature woman in wide pants standing near a clean dressing room mirror, small thought-bubble-like mood without actual text, humorous but elegant.
Subject: woman in her 40s-50s, fully clothed, expressive but not exaggerated, holding a handbag and looking as if she just remembered a small practical issue.
Style/medium: soft watercolor line illustration, fashion magazine column, gentle ink lines, pale colors.
Composition/framing: horizontal-friendly with clean negative space for Japanese title text, no in-image text.
Avoid: restroom interior, underwear, exposed skin, medical diagram, product photo, logo, watermark, written characters.
