# 011 画像生成プロンプト

共通制約: 商品画像、商品名、価格、型番、ロゴ、日本語テキストは背景に入れない。人物は服を着た自然な生活シーン。顔アップ、下着姿、露出、身体悩みの直接描写は禁止。010の明るい玄関・街歩き、012のコミカルイラストと似すぎないよう、落ち着いた外出先、手元、清潔感、裏方の気配を中心にする。

## 011_title_bg.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Rakuten content title background, later composed as 1600 x 900
Scene/backdrop: elegant hotel powder room entrance or clean public lounge corridor, no restroom fixture visible, tote bag and coat on a bench, mature wide pants outfit suggested naturally
Subject: tasteful everyday outing scene for a woman in her 40s-50s, fully clothed, back or side silhouette only
Style/medium: refined women's magazine editorial photography
Composition/framing: horizontal-friendly with clean negative space for Japanese title text, no in-image text
Lighting/mood: calm, clean, quietly practical, sophisticated
Avoid: toilet bowl, stall interior, product photo, underwear scene, face close-up, logos, text
