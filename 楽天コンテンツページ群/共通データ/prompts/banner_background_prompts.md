# 差し込みバナー背景プロンプト

## 基本方針

- 横長 1600px × 520px を標準にする。
- 背景画像は生成し、日本語テキストは後から正確に合成する。
- 本文3から5ブロックごとに1枚差し込む。
- 文章だけの離脱を防ぐため、情報整理と雰囲気作りを兼ねる。

## 共通スタイル

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: Rakuten content page insert banner background  
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial apparel lifestyle photography  
Composition/framing: horizontal banner composition, generous negative space for overlay text  
Lighting/mood: airy natural daylight, calm, reassuring, sophisticated  
Color palette: ivory, blush pink, taupe, muted burgundy accents  
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no medical or hospital feeling  
Avoid: cluttered layout, cartoon, distorted hands, garish colors, hard sales flyer look

## Banner 1: 悩み共感

Primary request: elegant lifestyle banner background for `{main_problem}`, no visible text  
Scene/backdrop: bright lifestyle setting related to the product category and daily use  
Subject: partial hands or everyday objects that make the scene concrete  
Text overlay later: `{problem_banner_title}` / `{problem_banner_body}`

## Banner 2: 選び方早見

Primary request: refined banner background about choosing by `{selection_axis}`, no visible text  
Scene/backdrop: neatly separated groups that visually suggest comparison  
Subject: category-relevant items arranged in 3 groups  
Text overlay later: comparison cards such as `{guide_item_1}`, `{guide_item_2}`, `{guide_item_3}`

## Banner 3: 素材・使用感

Primary request: tactile close-up banner background for `{material_or_comfort_point}`, no visible text  
Scene/backdrop: close-up texture, soft fabric or product material, gentle hands if appropriate  
Subject: material detail, no face, no body exposure  
Text overlay later: `{material_banner_title}` / `{material_banner_body}`

## Banner 4: CTA

Primary request: elegant final call-to-action banner background for `{content_title}`, no visible text  
Scene/backdrop: bright organized lifestyle scene leading naturally to product selection  
Subject: neatly arranged items, soft flowers, product category hint  
Text overlay later: `{cta_banner_title}` / `{cta_banner_body}`
